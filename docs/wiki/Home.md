**saywho tells you who spoke when in a recording.** Give it a meeting, an interview, a podcast or a video, and it
shows each person's turns on a timeline, how long everyone talked, when people talked over each other, and lets you
download the result as subtitles or a spreadsheet.

It runs **on your own computer**. Your recordings never leave it.

![saywho with a sample recording](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anand34577/saywho/main/docs/images/screenshot.png)

## What it can and can't do

| saywho does | saywho doesn't |
|---|---|
| Find where each speaker talks, up to 8 people | Write down what was said (that's transcription, a different tool) |
| Show talk time, turns, overlaps and interruptions | Tell you who a person is: speakers are "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", which you can rename |
| Export subtitles (SRT), a spreadsheet (CSV), RTTM and JSON | Need an internet connection once it's installed |
| Work with almost any audio or video file | Send your recordings anywhere |

## What do I need?

- A 64-bit computer with at least **4 GB of memory** (8 GB is more comfortable) and about **5 GB of free disk
  space** (12 GB if you have an NVIDIA graphics card).
- An internet connection during installation, to download saywho and its model (about 400 MB).
- An NVIDIA graphics card makes it about four times faster, but it isn't required. A normal laptop processor handles an
  hour of audio in a couple of minutes.
