# Sample recording

`earnest.flac` is a 74-second excerpt (16 kHz mono) of Act 1 of *The Importance of Being Earnest* by Oscar Wilde,
from the LibriVox dramatic reading "The Importance of Being Earnest (version 3)":
<https://archive.org/details/importanceofbeingearnest_1205_librivox>, file
`importanceofbeingearnest_1_wilde_64kb.mp3`, from 3:34.7 to 4:48.3.

It has three voices: two cast members reading Algernon and Jack, and the narrator reading the stage directions.
saywho finds 3 speakers and 26 segments in it with the default settings.

**License.** LibriVox recordings are in the public domain. This one is marked with the
[Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/), and the play itself (1895) is in the
public domain. The excerpt is redistributed here unchanged, apart from conversion to FLAC and short fades at the
start and end. Thanks to the LibriVox volunteers who recorded it.

The UI's **Try the sample** button and `python app.py --check` use this file.
